About Flex Cuts

FlexCuts is a local barbershop in South Osborne, Winnipeg, specializing in clean, precise and personalized haircuts. From sharp skin fades and taper fades to classic men’s cuts, kids’ haircuts, senior cuts and beard grooming, our barbers focus on detail, consistency and making every client leave looking their best.

Conveniently located at 660 Osborne Street, we proudly welcome clients from South Osborne and surrounding Winnipeg neighbourhoods.

Walk-ins are welcome, and appointments can be booked online.
